SEO 101

Learn Off-Page SEO

Start with the building blocks. No complicated jargon required.

01

What is Off-Page SEO?

Off-page SEO includes activities outside your website that can influence how your site is discovered, referenced and perceived online. Link building is a major part, but it is not the whole story.

02

What is a Backlink?

A backlink is a hyperlink on one website that points to another website. For example, if a blog links to your article as a useful reference, your article receives a backlink.

03

Anchor Text

Anchor text is the clickable text of a link. Descriptive, natural anchor text can help users understand what they will find after clicking.

04

Dofollow & Nofollow

Links can carry attributes such as rel="nofollow", rel="sponsored" or rel="ugc". These attributes give search engines context about the relationship with the linked page.

05

Guest Posting

Guest posting means contributing useful content to another publication. Focus on relevance and genuine value rather than publishing low-quality articles only to obtain links.

06

Digital PR

Original research, useful data, expert commentary and newsworthy stories can earn mentions from journalists, publications and communities.

💡 Beginner rule: A backlink is not automatically a “good backlink.” Consider relevance, quality, context, editorial intent and whether the link is genuinely useful to people.
